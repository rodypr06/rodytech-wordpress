Application policy still needs its own checks: confirm the caller's trusted tenant, the target record's permissions and any required business approval. Test a permitted read, a denied write and an attempted cross-tenant action. Record the outcome without logging credentials. These are proposed acceptance checks, not results from a tested deployment.
