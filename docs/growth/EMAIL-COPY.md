## Reusable digest structure

Subject: a specific problem or useful outcome, without an unsupported promise.
Preheader: what the reader can use.
Opening: the decision or problem in two or three sentences.
Main item: one article and why it matters.
Try this: one practical action or resource.
Reply invitation: one question tied to the topic.
Footer: preference controls, unsubscribe, business identity and address.

Send only to confirmed subscribers. Record campaign ID, final subject, segment, reviewer and send result.
