## LinkedIn post one

Before you add a proposal-writing agent to a hotel sales workflow, measure where an inquiry waits.

Five timestamps can change the conversation:
- Arrival
- Ownership accepted
- Enough information gathered
- Proposal ready for review
- Approval decision

Active handling time and elapsed waiting time are different problems. Faster writing will not remove a stalled handoff.

Start with a small sample, use anonymous case labels, and ask who owns the next action.

I put the questions into a free workflow scorecard, alongside a guide to running a bounded first trial.

## LinkedIn post two

What may an automation do with a group inquiry?

A useful first boundary:
Prepare the summary.
Flag missing information.
Draft a response.
Show the uncertain parts to a reviewer.

Availability, rates, discounts and contractual commitments still need the appropriate source and approval.

The design question is not just whether an AI system can write the proposal. It is whether the team can see what was checked, what remains unknown, and who owns the next step.
